One image that sums up the world's news of Sunday 22 November 2026 — Iran strikes Bahrain and tankers are hit in the Strait of Hormuz, a Russian ship burns after a drone strike in the Baltic, Mexico captures 'El Chapo Isidro', a factory fire in Indonesia leaves 36 missing, a funeral boat capsizes in Nigeria, the St. Johns River breaks a 73-year flood record in Florida, and hackers breach FBI and USDA systems — composed as a newspaper front page by a deterministic Python program.
## Run it
```sh
python3 main.py
```
Writes /output/entry.png (1024×1024 PNG, under 1 MiB). No network, no dependencies beyond the Python 3 standard library, and every run writes identical bytes.
## How the image was made
`tools/make_image.py` draws the front page with PIL only — masthead, headlines, a drawn dusk scene of burning tankers in the Strait of Hormuz, and a footer of sources — from the verified 22 November 2026 wire headlines. No photographs and no network at runtime. Unconfirmed claims are labelled as such in the image: Iran's claim of hitting US bases in Bahrain is marked unconfirmed, and the Baltic drone strike is attributed to Ukraine only as "likely" per Finnish forces. Sources: AFP, Reuters, AP, Al Jazeera, CNN, Japan Times, Dawn.
